# LLM handoff — Seaton Sluice cardinal-arrow edition v8

## Outcome

V8 retains the approved v7 A3 map, extent, title and plotting-safe one-line
coordinates. Its only visual change is the final compass cell: the diamond has
been removed and replaced by an upward open north arrow whose vertical shaft
is intersected by one horizontal west-east axis. Shaft, arrowhead and axis are
separate non-retracing physical paths on the black 0.25 mm pen.

## Non-negotiable decisions

1. Keep the approved A3 city header, full-width field and v6 geographic crop.
2. Keep the `north-arrow-east-west-axis` compass style. Do not restore the
   diamond. Keep the shaft, open arrowhead and horizontal axis as separate
   non-retracing strokes with proportions from `city_header.compass_geometry`.
3. Keep the coordinate copy on one line in `city_coordinates`; never stack it.
4. Keep the three-space separator and 0.25 mm tracking. Both are generated by
   `tools/build_format_spec.py`, not private renderer constants.
5. Keep the north mark in its separate final cell on the black 0.25 mm pen.
6. Keep `way/60578761` as a required landmark reference. A render that cannot
   prove that exact Hall footprint is present must fail.
7. Keep every point of all five named Seaton Burn source ways inside the
   resolved extent. Do not infer or draw a Dene walking route.
8. Keep A3 landscape `city-map`, `plotter-faithful`, 0.04 mm simplification,
   centreline roads, dotted water, landmark-only buildings and actual pens.
9. Keep attribution external only while `ATTRIBUTION.md` accompanies every
   public copy.
10. Do not enable execution or accept physical conflicts without calibrated
   pens/stock, calibrated machine timing and human review.

## Reproduction

`docs/rebuild.sh` regenerates the master SVG, manifest, 11 per-pen SVGs,
254-DPI PNG, portable simulator and plot job from the pinned source without a
network request. It then runs the binding format and release verifiers and
seals `CHECKSUMS.sha256`.
