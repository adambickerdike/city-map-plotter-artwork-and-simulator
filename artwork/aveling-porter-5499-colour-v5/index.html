Coloured with pen lines

Every outline of the refined revision-14 drawing is black, with the engine's linework drawn one weight heavier so it holds its own beside the colour, and the regulator rod redrawn straight and level. The brass boiler bands are solid gold between their black edges, from end to end. The wheels, hubs and flywheel are black iron drawn as close, even circles; behind the rear wheel everything up to the tender's straight front edge is a lighter black, and the green tender carries on beyond it.

The fork and scrapers are red-brown, red lines darkened with brown, with green tender around the rear scraper; every spoke is ruled with the same number of lines, and the worksplate and horse at the top right are left as clean black engraving.

Made for real pens

Five pen loads: Gold 0.40 mm, then Green, Red, Brown and Black, all 0.25 mm. Every black line is the one fine black pen; the heavier outlines are built from overlapping 0.25 mm strokes, so no thick black pen is needed. The iron is hatched in black lines only, so no grey pen is needed. Every gold part is built from several fine gold lines, so no broad gold pen is needed. Colour lines stay at least 0.22 mm clear of black ink, so inks never mix; only the bands' thin gold inner lines run up to the black lines they join, as in the blueprint.

The optimised simulation runs about 50 minutes.
