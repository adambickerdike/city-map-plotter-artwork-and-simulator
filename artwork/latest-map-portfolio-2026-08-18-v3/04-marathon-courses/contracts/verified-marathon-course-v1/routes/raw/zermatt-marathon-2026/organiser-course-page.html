Marathon

The supreme discipline

Starting in the deepest valley in Switzerland in St. Niklaus and finishing on the Riffelberg or Gornergrat, the Zermatt Marathon is the alpine running challenge par excellence and one of the most demanding marathon routes in Europe.

Marathon

The mountain classic with a view of the Matterhorn

The Gornergrat Zermatt Marathon is considered one of the most beautiful and challenging mountain marathons in the world. On July 4, 2026, the route leads from St. Niklaus in the Mattertal valley up to the Riffelberg at 2,585 m above sea level - always with a view of the majestic Matterhorn in Zermatt.

The start and finish could not be more different

Over 42.195 kilometers, the Zermatt Marathon connects the valley floor of the Mattertal with the high alpine world of the Riffelberg. Several sections of the route lead through traditional villages, along mountain streams and along paths with stunning views. Year after year, the event attracts running enthusiasts from all over the world.

GZM Marathon 2026.gpx

Marathon highlights

  • From the valley floor in St. Niklaus to the Riffelberg at 2,585 m - with a view of the Matterhorn
  • 2,075 meters of ascent through picturesque Valais mountain villages
  • Unique mountain scenery and unforgettable atmosphere along the route
  • High-quality finisher shirt and a medal
  • Free arrival and return journey (Swiss Runners Ticket)
