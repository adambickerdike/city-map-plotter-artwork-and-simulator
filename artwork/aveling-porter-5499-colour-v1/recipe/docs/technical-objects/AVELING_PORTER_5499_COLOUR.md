# Aveling & Porter No. 5499 - colour edition 1

Package: `examples/technical-objects/aveling-porter-5499-colour-v1/`.
Builder: `tools/engineering_source_plates/build_aveling_5499_colour.py`.
Policy and verifier: `tools/engineering_source_plates/aveling_5499_colour_v1/`.

The source is the hash-pinned final revision-14 SVG, not a new reconstruction.
All 863 paths are retained. The engine contours, horse, lettering and layout
are identical; only three straight boiler-band centre-lines have 0.80 mm
endpoint insets to accommodate the Gold 1.00 mm pen. The centreline stations
remain fixed. The short upper strap fragment stays fine Black 0.25 mm.

The actual No. 5499 photographs by Benjamin Matthews and Terry Pinnegar guide
Green cladding/spokes, Red frames and scrapers, Black mechanics and Gold brass
accents. Studio ink colours are illustrative equivalents, not measured paint
matches. The detailed horse stays Black 0.25/0.40 mm because a 1 mm gold nib
would obscure its reliefs. Fine black lettering is fully retained.

The A3 layout uses the same user-authorised heritage zones and compact footer.
Paper is white; the original blueprint remains a separate unchanged edition.
The eight physical pens are Gold 1.00, Green 0.25/0.40, Red 0.25/0.40,
Black 0.25/0.40/0.60 mm. No custom or fictitious nib sizes are introduced.

Verification binds each path to the source and checks the new pen inventory,
widths, single-pass geometry, eight-nib cap heights, three-nib path lengths,
complete 12-block lettering, gold rim clearance, 8 equivalent pen files and
272 format checks. The accepted engine joins, circular features and occlusion
are inherited through unchanged geometry from the retained revision-14 audit.

The nominal optimised simulation is 15:27, with 8 pen loads and 8.78 m pen-up
travel versus 21.8 m in document order. No machine is operated. The package
includes A3 PDF, SVG/PNG, separate pen layers, simulator, references, comparison,
checksums and a portable rebuild snapshot. The colour version is published
alongside the existing blueprint in the artwork-and-simulator repository.
