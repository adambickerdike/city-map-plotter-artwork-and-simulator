A clearer silhouette and sculpted detail

The supplied photograph guides the face, ears, mane, muscular body, joints, hooves and tail. The casting is turned upright and uniformly fitted to the accepted top-right space.

The photograph and the exact drawing curves

The outline is fitted as smooth cubic curves after isolating and smoothing the brass silhouette. Interior reliefs are redrafted from the photographed casting and spaced for the actual nibs. The orientation is a presentation rotation, not a measured perspective correction.

The final emblem is 45.54 mm high, using a 0.40 mm outline and 0.30 mm details. All reliefs lie within the silhouette, without crossed strokes. The engine, plaque, lettering and border retain all 823 previously accepted non-horse paths exactly.
