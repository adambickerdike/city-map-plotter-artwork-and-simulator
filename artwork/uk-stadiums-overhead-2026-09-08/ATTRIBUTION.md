Where the retained source manifests credit Environment Agency survey data,
that data is under the Open Government Licence v3.0.

The stadium linework is a simplified, authored illustration of main overhead
features, reviewed against aerial references. It is not a measured as-built
survey. Original source identifiers, hashes, reference links, phase caveats
and validation are retained in the accompanying manifests.

Google Maps and Esri photographs were used for private visual comparison.
No reference screenshots, satellite images, aerial comparison boards or
generated raster stadium masters are distributed in this package. The
included PNGs are previews of the delivered vector artwork. Reference links
and metadata do not grant permission to redistribute third-party imagery.

public reuse, for example in the product listing or accompanying packaging.
