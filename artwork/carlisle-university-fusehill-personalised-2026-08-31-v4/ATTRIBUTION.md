The clean artwork uses external attribution by design. This file, or an
equivalent visible attribution on the product page or packaging, must accompany

The printed institution name was checked against the University of Cumbria's
official website and its official Carlisle campus pages on 30 August 2026.
Those pages establish the institution name and Fusehill Street campus identity
only; OpenStreetMap supplies the plotted geographic geometry.
